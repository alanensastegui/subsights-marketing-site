## Meet Sunny, Visit Sun Valley's website assistant

Visit Sun Valley uses Subsights to help visitors find information about its destination. Sunny gives people another way to explore the website: they can ask a question in their own words and continue the conversation as their plans take shape.

## Built around the destination

A destination website covers many different needs, from things to do and places to stay to events and local logistics. Sunny brings that content into a conversational experience, with links that help visitors continue exploring.

The setup includes the assistant's look, greeting, tone, sources and guidance. Subsights works with the destination team to test useful questions and improve the experience.

## What the customer reported

Jessica Maynard, Director of Operations at Visit Sun Valley, described the experience this way:

> Lucas and the Subsights team have been great to work with from the start. They have always been available and helpful.
>
> The chatbot is fantastic in providing eloquent and informative responses to users. We have seen a decrease in email inquiries as a result. Lastly, I love that the chatbot can speak many different languages!

This is the customer's own description of the benefit. We have not assigned a percentage to the email reduction or attributed bookings to the assistant.

## Another source of visitor insight

Conversations help the team see what visitors are trying to find. Reviewing questions and the links people use can inform website content, seasonal guidance and future improvements.
