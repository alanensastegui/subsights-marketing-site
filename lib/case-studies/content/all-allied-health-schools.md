## A past implementation for All Star Directories

Subsights previously worked with All Star Directories on All Allied Health Schools. The project connected the site's educational content and approved affiliate links in a conversational experience.

This is a historical implementation, not a current customer endorsement.

## From a broad question to a relevant next step

Readers arrive with different goals, locations and levels of experience. The assistant used website content to explain options and surface relevant partner links within the conversation.

The engineering work included retaining approved affiliate destinations and their tracking parameters when presenting links.

## Fit matters before the click

A useful recommendation needs to make sense for the reader. The experience should clarify what someone is trying to do, explain relevant options and make it clear when a suitable match is not established.

That lesson shapes how we approach new publisher and directory projects. Live program availability, provider eligibility and catalog connections need their own agreed integration.

## What engagement reporting can tell you

Conversations and link clicks can show whether readers engage with an option. They do not establish accepted leads, applications, enrollments or revenue. Those outcomes need data from the publisher's downstream systems.
