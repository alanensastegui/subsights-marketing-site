## Meet Penny at Intrust Funding

Intrust Funding uses Subsights for its website assistant, Penny. The implementation is built around the lender's Washington real estate investor focus and the questions people ask before contacting the lending team.

## Help visitors understand the next step

A borrower might arrive knowing what they want to buy or renovate, but still need to understand the lender's service area, program focus or application process.

The assistant gives visitors a place to ask those questions. Its sources, instructions and contact paths are configured around the business, rather than a generic lending script.

## Keep the team's role clear

An assistant can explain approved information and guide someone to the right application or person. Current terms, underwriting, approvals and project-specific decisions belong with the lending team.

Connections that read or change a CRM, application or loan record require their own agreed scope. A website assistant by itself does not establish that those systems are connected.

## Learn from the questions

Conversation review can help identify recurring questions about program fit, geography and application requirements. Those findings can guide clearer website content and better first conversations.
