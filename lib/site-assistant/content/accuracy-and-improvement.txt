Subsights provides conversations for review, feedback and insights. Useful improvements start with real questions and follow-ups: inspect a wrong or unanswered response, flag or comment on the conversation, correct the source website or approved chatbot guidance, and retest the question and nearby cases. A visitor's assertion does not become a verified fact or a live instruction automatically.
Conversation history does not automatically retrain the assistant or publish a correction. A proposed workflow where report feedback automatically becomes a reviewed change is not a generally released feature. Managed review and content updates are the current improvement path. AI answers can be wrong; testing, clear limitations and source links matter. Do not guarantee 98% accuracy, perfect answers or that a report will catch every mistake.
Website recrawling and calendar freshness depend on configured sources and successful access. A blocked or failed crawl can leave a gap or stale content. "Connected" does not mean the assistant reads every page at the moment of each visitor question.
