Subsights is a managed AI website assistant. It answers visitors' questions from approved business content, can guide them to relevant pages and provides a record of the questions people ask. Setup includes choosing sources, tone, appearance and boundaries, testing realistic questions and adding the widget to the website. Managed support is included in the published paid plans. Visitors do not need to share contact details just to get answers. Optional lead capture is separate from the main answering experience.
