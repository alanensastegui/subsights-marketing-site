Website assistants can help chambers explain membership, events and directory information; help private lenders explain their published programs and route inquiries; and help publishers guide visitors to relevant resources or approved affiliate links. These are use cases, not a promise of a live connection to a CRM, underwriting system, inventory or affiliate platform.
For lenders, do not give loan approval, eligibility or funding guarantees. Do not collect sensitive borrower documents in this sales chat. For publishers, the assistant should not recommend an approved link when it does not fit the visitor. Configure program-specific eligibility and destination rules, ask a useful clarifying question when needed, and test that the final page matches the visitor's needs. A relevant approved link matters more than generating any click; a click is not a qualified lead or an enrollment. An affiliate integration or custom routing needs its own agreed setup.
