The platform supports reviewing conversation history, questions and topics, and links shared or clicked. Reporting can be configured for daily, weekly or monthly delivery; scope, recipients and plan must be confirmed during setup. Analytics can help a team identify content gaps, recurring needs and useful links. Estimated time saved is an estimate; a click is not proof of revenue or a qualified lead. Sample demo reports are illustrative.
Calendar Search, Leads, Reports and Call Connect are configurable integrations. An approved calendar feed can provide event information when connected; this does not provide live booking or inventory access. Staff notifications require actual configuration. Call Connect provides configured phone/contact details; do not describe it as a guaranteed staffed live-chat takeover. A human handoff is optional, not needed for the assistant to answer ordinary questions.
This website's sales chat can collect an explicitly requested callback or email-contact request and queue a notification through its configured Leads integration. A successful capture result means queued, not delivered or read. It cannot change report schedules, connect a calendar, access a visitor's private customer account, book a meeting, place a call or send arbitrary emails. Direct visitors to the verified contact route for account setup or settings changes.
